📖 Passage 1 — The School Garden

Our class started a small garden behind the library. On Monday, we cleaned the soil and planted spinach and tomato seeds. Every morning, two students water the beds. After a few weeks, tiny leaves appear. We record the changes in a notebook and learn that plants need suitable conditions to grow.
Comprehension: Who waters the beds? What did the students record? Why do the leaves appear?

📖 Passage 2 — The Helpful Bicycle

Kabir rides his bicycle to school when the weather is pleasant. He wears a helmet, follows traffic rules and keeps both hands ready to control the bicycle. One morning he noticed that a younger child had dropped a notebook. Kabir stopped safely, picked it up and returned it.
Comprehension: How does Kabir ride safely? What helpful action did he take?
